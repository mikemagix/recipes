# Chickpea Chocolate Chip Cookies

![Chickpea Chocolate Chip Cookies](chickpea-chocolate-chip-cookies.jpg)

Soft chocolate chip cookies made with cooked chickpeas, peanut butter and honey — no flour, blended in one bowl.

## Ingredients (full recipe)

- 1 cup or 170 g cooked chickpeas
- 1/3 cup or 80 g peanut butter
- 2 tbsp or 30 g honey
- 1 tsp or 5 g vanilla extract (5 ml)
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/3 cup or 50 g dark chocolate chips

## Method

1. Blend chickpeas, peanut butter, honey, vanilla, and baking powder until smooth.
2. Transfer to a bowl and mix in chocolate chips.
3. Scoop onto a lined baking tray and flatten slightly.
4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10–12 minutes until lightly golden.
5. Let cool for a few minutes before eating.
