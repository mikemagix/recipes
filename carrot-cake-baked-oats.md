# Carrot Cake Baked Oats

![Carrot Cake Baked Oats](carrot-cake-baked-oats.jpg)

Źródło: [rolka na Facebooku](https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1DtVDhQDAX/)

## Ingredients

### Baked oats

- 2–3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 2 eggs
- ¾ cup milk of choice
- ½ cup Greek yogurt
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 2 scoops vanilla protein powder
- 1 cup grated carrot
- ½ cup chopped pecans or walnuts
- 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp ginger
- ¼ tsp nutmeg
- 1 tsp baking powder
- a pinch of salt

### Topping

- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp honey
- a splash of vanilla
- cinnamon

## Method

1. Blend the topping ingredients until smooth. Store it separately if preferred.
2. Mix all baked oats ingredients in an 8 × 8-inch dish.
3. Bake at **350°F / 175°C** for **30–35 minutes**.
4. Let cool, top, and slice into 4 servings.
