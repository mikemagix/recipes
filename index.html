Carrot Cake Baked Oats

Source: Facebook reel

Baked oats

  • 2–3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 eggs
  • ¾ cup milk of choice
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 2 scoops vanilla protein powder
  • 1 cup grated carrot
  • ½ cup chopped pecans or walnuts
  • 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ginger
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp baking powder and a pinch of salt

Topping

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • a splash of vanilla and cinnamon

Method

  1. Blend the topping ingredients until smooth.
  2. Mix the baked oats ingredients in an 8 × 8-inch dish.
  3. Bake at 175°C for 30–35 minutes.
  4. Let cool, top, and slice into 4 servings.

Courgette Chocolate Brownie Oats

Source: Facebook reel

A fudgy baked-oats brownie made with grated courgette.

Ingredients

  • 50 g oat flour
  • 80 g grated courgette
  • 30 g chocolate vegan protein powder
  • 5–10 g cocoa powder
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 170 ml water or milk
  • Chocolate for the top

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix all ingredients together.
  3. Pour into an oven-safe ramekin and top with chocolate.
  4. Bake for 20–25 minutes.
  5. Top with Greek yogurt and peanut butter, if desired.

Apple Almond Caramel Oat Bars

Source: Facebook reel

An autumn snack made with apple, almond butter, oats, and a simple caramel topping.

Ingredients

  • 160 g oats
  • 120 g grated apple
  • 60 g almond butter
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 2 flax eggs
  • 50 ml syrup
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 30 g chopped walnuts
  • 220 ml milk

Caramel topping

  • 60 g almond butter
  • 20 ml maple syrup
  • a pinch of salt
  • 20 ml milk

Method

  1. Combine the oat-bar ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Bake in a lined square dish at 180°C / 350°F for 25–35 minutes.
  3. Cool for 10–15 minutes.
  4. Mix and spread the caramel topping, then cut into squares.

Single-Serve Apple Pie

Source: Facebook reel

A simple single-serving, no-knead apple crumble-style pie.

Ingredients

  • 25 g ground oats
  • 25 g semolina
  • 25 g erythritol
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 apples
  • ½–1 tsp cinnamon
  • 10 g butter
  • 150 ml water

Method

  1. Mix the dry ingredients.
  2. Simmer grated apples with cinnamon, erythritol, and water for 5 minutes.
  3. Layer the dry mixture and apples in three and two layers respectively.
  4. Top with grated cold butter.
  5. Bake at 180°C for about 30 minutes, or air-fry for 20–25 minutes.
