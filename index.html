Carrot Cake Baked Oats

Source: Facebook reel

Baked oats

  • 2–3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 eggs
  • ¾ cup milk of choice
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 2 scoops vanilla protein powder
  • 1 cup grated carrot
  • ½ cup chopped pecans or walnuts
  • 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ginger
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp baking powder and a pinch of salt

Topping

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • a splash of vanilla and cinnamon

Method

  1. Blend the topping ingredients until smooth.
  2. Mix the baked oats ingredients in an 8 × 8-inch dish.
  3. Bake at 175°C for 30–35 minutes.
  4. Let cool, top, and slice into 4 servings.
